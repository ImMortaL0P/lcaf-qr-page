What is
Laxmi Foods?

Born from the royal confectionery and snack traditions of India, Laxmi Confectionery & Foods (LCAF) handcrafts authentic namkeens using desert moth bean flour, stone-crushed spices, and virgin cold-pressed groundnut oil. Every batch honors century-old recipes with pure acoustic crunch in every bite.

Why Choose
Our Crunch?

We say an uncompromising NO to Palm Oil. While commercial brands cut corners with cheap blends, we fry and roast in pure cold-pressed groundnut and olive oils. Zero trans-fat, zero cholesterol, and verified digital nutritional transparency accessible right from our QR codes.

When to
Enjoy Namkeen?

From morning cutting chai with spicy Ratlami Sev, to festive Diwali gatherings with Shahi Navratan Mixture, or midnight guilt-free munching with Himalayan Pink Salt Makhana. Every moment becomes a royal celebration of crunch.

TRADITION & HERITAGE

The Secret of
Pure Desi Spices

We don't buy bulk factory-blended masalas. At Laxmi Confectionery & Foods, whole cloves, dried mango, cumin, and black peppercorns are stone-crushed daily in our kitchen kadhais to preserve natural essential oils. Combined with zero palm oil, our namkeens deliver an authentic crunch that never coats your palate with grease.
